About / 关于

A design practice for identity, place and cultural exchange.

面向品牌、空间与文化交流的设计实践。

Studio Signo works across brand identity, place and wayfinding, and cross-cultural strategy to make ideas legible across contexts.

Studio Signo 专注品牌识别、空间导视与跨文化策略，让设计在不同文化语境中清晰发生。
